# Papyrd Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026.

Papyrd is a free, open-source ebook reader for user-selected OPDS libraries and
optional KOSync reading-progress synchronization. There are no advertisements,
in-app purchases, developer analytics, tracking, or developer-operated telemetry
in the app. The developer does not collect personal information through the app.

## Information on your device

Papyrd stores downloaded books, reading progress, reader preferences, server
addresses, and any credentials you enter in its local app storage. These support
reading and connecting to your servers; they are not sent to the developer.
Device backups may include app data according to your operating-system settings.
Protect your device and use dedicated, limited-access server credentials.

## Connections you choose

When you configure an OPDS library, Papyrd connects directly to that library and
its linked content servers to browse catalogs, retrieve covers, and download
books. Requests may include the credentials you provide. Like other network
services, those servers can see your IP address and the resources you request.

KOSync is disabled by default. If you enable it, Papyrd sends your chosen server
an account username and authentication key, a document identifier, reading
position and percentage, and device name and identifier. Signing in may create
an account on that server if one does not already exist. The suggested public
KOSync server is operated independently of Papyrd; you can replace it with your
own compatible server.

Server operators control their own logging, retention, and privacy practices.
Self-hosting lets you control those practices yourself. Use trusted servers and
HTTPS. Papyrd does not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

## Removing information

You can remove downloaded books and change or remove configured connections in
the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to your device's
backup settings. Disabling synchronization stops future progress synchronization;
it does not delete information already stored on a server. Contact that server's
operator or use its administration tools to delete server-side data or accounts.

## Support and changes

If you voluntarily open a public GitHub issue, its contents are public and handled
by GitHub under its own privacy policy. Do not post passwords, authentication
keys, private server addresses, or personal information.

For questions, contact the maintainer through the
[Papyrd issue tracker](https://github.com/RileyMathews/papyrd-mobile/issues)
or email [dev@rileymathews.com](mailto:dev@rileymathews.com).
Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
