# Papyrd Support

Papyrd is a free, open-source ebook reader for iOS and Android. It connects to
your OPDS library and optionally synchronizes reading progress using KOSync.

## Getting started

1. Open Settings and add your OPDS server under OPDS settings.
2. Enter the catalog endpoint supplied by your server administrator (for example,
   `https://your-server.example/opds`) and credentials if required.
3. Open Browse, choose your server, and download a book to your Library.
4. Open the downloaded book to read. Downloaded books can be read offline.
5. Optionally configure a compatible KOSync server in Settings to synchronize
   progress. Synchronization is disabled by default.

Papyrd does not include a commercial book subscription or a hosted library.
Only access books you own or are otherwise legally permitted to read. Content
and its age suitability depend on the library you choose; Papyrd does not curate
or filter your library and is not a dedicated children's service.

## Contact and troubleshooting

[Open a support issue](https://github.com/RileyMathews/papyrd-mobile/issues/new)
to contact the maintainer, Riley Mathews. Include your app version, iOS/Android
version, server software/version, the error message, and steps to reproduce.
Check that your catalog endpoint is correct, your server is reachable from your
device, and your credentials have permission to browse and download books.

GitHub issues are public. Never include passwords, authentication keys, private
server addresses, or personal information. Use a temporary test account and
redact screenshots and logs before sharing them.

See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) and
[source code](https://github.com/RileyMathews/papyrd-mobile).
